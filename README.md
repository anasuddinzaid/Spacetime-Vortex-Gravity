## Abstract

Gravity is traditionally explained by General Relativity as the curvature of spacetime due to mass-energy. However, this theory does not explain the *mechanism* behind why space bends. In this paper, I propose a new theory — **Spacetime Vortex Gravity (SVG)** — where gravity originates not from curvature alone, but from **rotational vortex-like motion of spacetime** caused by mass. This rotational influence produces attractive effects analogous to gravitational force, and provides a physical mechanism that explains why spacetime curves. SVG also challenges the **block universe model** and supports a universe with a dynamic flow of time and potential for **free will**. This theory aims to extend Einstein’s framework by introducing a deeper cause for gravitation grounded in vortex dynamics of spacetime.

---

## 1. The Core Idea of Spacetime Vortex Gravity (SVG)

In SVG, gravity is the result of a rotational distortion — a vortex — in spacetime itself. Rather than viewing mass as simply causing spacetime curvature, SVG proposes that mass induces a **local spin or swirl** in spacetime. This vortex structure influences nearby masses, pulling them toward the center of the vortex. This rotational field behaves analogously to the way water pulls objects toward a whirlpool, or how frame-dragging slightly twists space in General Relativity — except in SVG, **this vortex is the *primary cause* of gravitational attraction**, not a side effect.

This concept replaces “passive curvature” with **active spacetime flow**, meaning mass creates a dynamic twist, not a static bend.

---

## 2. Time, Free Will, and the Block Universe

Einstein's relativity suggests that all points in time exist equally — past, present, future — forming a "block universe." This removes the possibility of true free will or dynamic flow of time.

SVG proposes that if spacetime is actively rotating (not just geometrically curving), then the passage of time may emerge from **angular dynamics of spacetime flow**, making the present moment physically unique. This opens a path to restore **free will** in a physical framework.

---

## 3. Vision and Future Work

The goal of SVG is not to discard Einstein’s work, but to extend it by explaining *why* gravity arises. Future work will involve:

- Mathematical modeling of vortex-induced spacetime curvature
- Comparing predictions of SVG with General Relativity
- Exploring implications for galaxy rotation, dark matter, and time asymmetry
